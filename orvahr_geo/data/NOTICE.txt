Upstream states its postcodes come from the Thailand Post poster
"รหัสไปรษณีย์ทั่วประเทศ จำแนกตามท้องที่" (May 2018); the area codes are the
DOPA administrative codes.

Transformation: names whitespace-normalised; codes as strings; every
postcode of a subdistrict kept (space separated, primary first). Counts:
77 provinces, 928 districts, 7,436 subdistricts, 974 distinct postcodes,
86 subdistricts with more than one postcode.

To refresh: rebuild th_geo.json in the same format, then run
`python manage.py load_th_geo` (idempotent; never deletes rows).
